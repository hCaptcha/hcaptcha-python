# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful and productive community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Do not share private data, credentials, or hCaptcha response tokens.
- Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against other people.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to support@hcaptcha.com. Do not post sensitive details in a public GitHub issue.

## Enforcement

hCaptcha maintainers review reports and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.
